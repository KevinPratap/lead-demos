Meet the Doctor

Expert Physiotherapy for a Healthier, Pain-Free Life

Dr. Tanvi Yash Shah (BPT) is the founder and head physiotherapist at Physio-Fit. She earned her Bachelor of Physiotherapy from the prestigious **D.Y. Patil College of Physiotherapy, Mumbai**, and has over 5 years of clinical and home rehabilitation experience.

Dr. Tanvi is a **Certified Cupping Therapist, Certified Dry Needling Practitioner, and Certified Aquatic Therapist**. Her clinical model prioritizes active, exercise-based recovery, core alignment, and manual mobilization to treat structural issues rather than relying on passive machine therapies.

How We Work

Our 3-Step Recovery Process

01

Movement Assessment

Dr. Tanvi Shah performs a detailed physical evaluation and postural assessment to identify muscle imbalances and determine the root cause of your pain.

02

Custom Treatment Plan

A tailored recovery schedule combining manual therapy, certified needling/cupping, and orthopedic muscle conditioning fits your specific goals.

03

Rebuild & Prevention

Active strengthening exercises, stretching habits, and posture training to restore full mobility, build resilience, and prevent pain from returning.
