Meet the Doctor

Expert Physiotherapy for a Healthier, Pain-Free Life

Dr. Tanvi Yash Shah (BPT) is the founder and head clinician at Physio-Fit. She earned her Bachelor of Physiotherapy from the prestigious **D.Y. Patil College of Physiotherapy, Mumbai**, and has spent over 5 years treating orthopedic, musculoskeletal, and neurological cases.

She is a **Certified Cupping Therapist, Certified Dry Needling Practitioner, and Certified Aquatic Therapist**. Dr. Tanvi's clinical focus centers on biomechanical education and exercise-based adjustment, helping patients identify the root cause of their joint pain rather than relying on temporary machine release.
