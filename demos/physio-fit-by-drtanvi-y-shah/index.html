Meet the Doctor

Dr. Tanvi Yash Shah (BPT)

Dr. Tanvi Yash Shah completed her Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) from D.Y. Patil College of Physiotherapy, Mumbai. She is a dedicated clinician with specialized certifications in Cupping Therapy, Dry Needling, and Aquatic Therapy.

Her clinical philosophy focuses on educational empowerment and exercise-based biomechanical correction, helping patients understand their symptoms to prevent recurrences. She conducts treatments both at her Vile Parle clinic and through home visits for bedridden or post-surgical patients.
