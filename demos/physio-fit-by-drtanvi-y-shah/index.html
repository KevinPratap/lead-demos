Meet Dr. Tanvi Shah

Clinical Expertise for a Healthier, Pain-Free Life

Dr. Tanvi Yash Shah (BPT) is the founder and head therapist at Physio-Fit. She completed her clinical graduation from the prestigious D.Y. Patil College of Physiotherapy, Mumbai, and has spent over five years managing complex orthopedic, musculoskeletal, and active sports injuries.

Dr. Tanvi's treatment philosophy focuses on patient biomechanical education and manual corrective adjustments over simple passive machines. She holds specialized clinical certifications to provide high-quality targeted therapies.
