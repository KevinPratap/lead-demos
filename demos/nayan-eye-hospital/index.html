Welcome to Nayan Eye Hospital

Preserving and restoring sight with clinical precision and compassionate medical care

Nayan Eye Hospital is Navi Mumbai's trusted center for advanced vision diagnostics and surgical care. Under the guidance of our lead ophthalmic surgeons, we specialize in high-precision treatments for cataract, glaucoma, retinal health, and pediatric eye diseases. Located in Sector 11, Ghansoli, our hospital combines state-of-the-art diagnostic imaging with outpatient surgery to provide a seamless clinical experience.
