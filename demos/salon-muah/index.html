About Salon Muah

Founded with a vision to redefine luxury hair and beauty styling, Salon Muah has been the premier fashion forward destination in Khar West, Mumbai for over ten years. We believe that hair and beauty are forms of personal expression, and our expert stylists curate bespoke designs that reflect your individuality.

With a stellar reputation of 4.7 stars across 636 reviews, our salon combines contemporary techniques with premium care. Our space is designed to feel like an editorial studio, offering a calm and luxurious escape from the bustling streets of Mumbai. Whether you seek a precision haircut, couture color, or advanced skincare, we commit to delivering a transformational experience.
